DRAFT PETITION · ORGANIZER CONTENT TO FOLLOW

Every person should be able to walk to work and return home safely.

We, the undersigned supporters of ONE VOICE, call on the responsible public authorities, road agencies, traffic enforcement bodies and transport operators to take urgent, measurable action to protect pedestrians in the Infopark–Kakkanad area.

WE CALL FOR

  1. Safe pedestrian access. Repair and protect continuous footpaths, remove dangerous obstructions and provide usable crossing points.
  2. Responsible bus operations. Enforce speed limits, dangerous-driving penalties and safe stopping practices.
  3. Engineering fixes. Audit high-risk junctions and walking routes, publish remedial actions and implement visible safety improvements.
  4. Public accountability. Publish a time-bound action plan with regular progress updates.

This is working campaign wording, not an official filed petition. Organizers will replace it with the approved text.
